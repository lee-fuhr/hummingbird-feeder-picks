Quick answer

For a budget-friendly glass hummingbird feeder that balances simplicity, capacity, and easy cleaning, the First Nature 3032 is our top recommendation.

Choose the Nature’s Way CST-3-13 if you prefer a smaller, sturdy design with a built-in ant moat, or the Perky-Pet 274-12 Antique if you want a classic look with a popular reputation.

A hummingbird feeder doesn’t need to cost a fortune to bring these dazzling visitors to your yard. Budget-friendly glass feeders can be just as effective as pricier models, as long as you focus on two things: easy disassembly for cleaning and a design that prevents leaks and drips. Mold is a serious health risk for hummers, so a feeder you can take apart and scrub without frustration is non-negotiable. When shopping on a budget, you’ll typically sacrifice a bit of durability or fancy features like built-in bee guards. But the best value models use tempered glass reservoirs, simple plastic bases that resist fading, and wide mouths for filling. We’ve gathered three well-reviewed glass feeders that each hit a sweet spot of cost and function, based on owner feedback and published expert reviews.
