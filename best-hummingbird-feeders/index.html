Quick answer

The Aspects 398 Hummzinger Ultra is the best glass hummingbird feeder for most people, balancing durability, easy cleaning, and reliable ant-moat defense.

Hummingbird feeders are a backyard staple, but the wrong design can turn a joy into a chore. Mold-prone crevices, hard-to-reach seams, and leaky ports make cleaning a hassle, and dirty feeders harm birds. A good feeder should disassemble quickly, expose every surface to a bottle brush, and use glass (not plastic) for lasting clarity and stain resistance. This round-up focuses on glass models that are genuinely easy to clean, based on owner feedback, expert reviews, and reputation for durability. We’ve ranked them for ease of maintenance, value, and how well they keep nectar fresh, so you can pick the one that fits your patio and your peace of mind.
